# Google Play listing (English)

## App name

Parrot Trainer

## Short description

Practice parrot speech with adjustable phrase repetition and sound response.

## Full description

Parrot Trainer is a simple training companion for practicing familiar words and
phrases with your bird. It listens for room sounds and can repeat a selected
training phrase after a short pause. When the room is quiet, it can also repeat
phrases at adjustable intervals.

Create your own phrase list, record phrases in your voice, adjust microphone
sensitivity, choose installed Android text-to-speech voices, set training hours,
and view local training statistics.

Your recorded phrases, settings, and training history are stored in the app's
local storage. Parrot Trainer has no developer-operated backend, advertising,
or analytics. Android speech-recognition and text-to-speech providers selected
on your device may process audio or phrase text according to their own privacy
terms.

Background training can continue with the screen off using an Android foreground
service with an ongoing notification, so the app can respond to sounds during an
active training session.

Parrot Trainer is a training aid. It does not guarantee that a parrot will
learn to speak; every bird learns differently.
