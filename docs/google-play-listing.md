# Google Play listing (English)

## App name

Parrot Trainer

## Short description

Practice parrot speech with adjustable phrase repetition and sound response.

## Full description

Parrot Trainer is a simple training companion for practicing familiar words and
phrases with your bird. It listens for room sounds and can repeat a selected
training phrase after a short pause. When the room is quiet, it can also repeat
phrases at adjustable intervals.

Create your own phrase list, record phrases in your voice, adjust microphone
sensitivity, choose installed Android text-to-speech voices, and view local
training statistics. Your phrases, recordings, settings, and statistics stay on
your device.

Parrot Trainer is a training aid. It does not guarantee that a parrot will
learn to speak; every bird learns differently.
