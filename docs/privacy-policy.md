# Privacy Policy — Parrot Trainer

Effective date: August 23, 2026

Parrot Trainer is developed by DimonSmart. Privacy questions can be submitted through the public issue tracker at https://github.com/DimonSmart/ParrotTrainer/issues.

## Data the app accesses

Parrot Trainer uses microphone access for its core training features:

- During training, microphone audio is used to measure sound level and detect when a sound starts and ends. Ambient monitoring audio is processed in memory by the app and is not intentionally saved by Parrot Trainer.
- When you explicitly record a training phrase, the resulting recording is stored in the app's private local storage on your device until you replace or delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
- If speech recognition is used while creating a voice phrase, the Android speech-recognition service selected on your device may process audio locally or may transmit it to that provider. That processing is controlled by the selected Android speech-recognition provider and is subject to its privacy terms.
- Text-to-speech is performed by the TTS engine selected on your Android device. Depending on that engine, phrase text may be processed locally or by the TTS provider under its own terms.

Training phrases, app settings, training statistics, and activity history are stored locally in app-private storage. Android may back up some settings and statistics according to the backup configuration of your device and backup provider. Parrot Trainer excludes recorded phrase audio from Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup.

## Collection and sharing by the developer

Parrot Trainer has no developer-operated backend, analytics service, advertising SDK, user account system, or in-app advertising. The developer does not receive or sell your microphone recordings, phrases, settings, or training statistics through the app.

Android system services or user-selected speech-recognition and TTS providers may process data as described above. Their processing is outside Parrot Trainer's servers because Parrot Trainer operates no server for these features.

## Data security

Data written by Parrot Trainer is stored in Android app-private storage and is protected by the security and application-sandbox mechanisms provided by Android. Parrot Trainer does not intentionally transmit its locally stored recordings, settings, or statistics to a developer-operated service.

## Retention and deletion

Locally stored data remains on the device until it is changed or deleted by the user or removed with the application data. You can:

- delete or replace individual recorded training phrases in the app;
- reset settings and statistics from the app;
- use Android's Clear storage / Clear data action to remove the app's local data; or
- uninstall the app.

Android-managed backups, if enabled, are controlled by Android and the user's backup provider. Recorded phrase audio is explicitly excluded from Parrot Trainer's Android backup rules.

## Permissions

The app requests microphone permission because sound detection, phrase recording, and optional speech recognition are core features. When background training is enabled, Android displays an ongoing foreground-service notification while microphone monitoring continues with the screen off.

## Children

Parrot Trainer is a general-purpose pet-training utility and does not intentionally collect personal information from children through a developer-operated service.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
