# Parrot Trainer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Parrot Trainer is developed by **DimonSmart**. This policy explains how Parrot Trainer accesses and handles user data.

## Data the app accesses

Parrot Trainer uses the microphone to detect sounds during a user-started training session. You can also record your own training phrases. The app stores phrase text, phrase recordings, settings, and training statistics in the app's private storage on your device.

The app does not operate a proprietary backend and does not upload this data to a DimonSmart server. It does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs, and DimonSmart does not sell user data.

## Android speech and text-to-speech services

When you use speech recognition, Parrot Trainer invokes the speech-recognition provider selected on your Android device. That provider may process or transmit audio according to its own privacy policy.

When Parrot Trainer speaks a phrase using an Android text-to-speech voice, the selected TTS provider processes the phrase text. Some voices may require network processing. Any such provider-side processing is governed by the selected provider's own privacy policy.

## Storage, backups, and security

Phrase recordings are kept in Android app-private storage and are excluded from the app's Android cloud-backup and device-transfer rules. App settings and statistics may be included in Android system backup if backup is enabled for your device and account; those backups are handled by the Android/Google backup service under its own terms and privacy policy.

Parrot Trainer does not intentionally transmit recordings, settings, or statistics to a developer-controlled server. Data that stays on the device is protected by Android's application sandbox. Where an Android speech or TTS provider transmits data, security in transit is controlled by that provider.

## Retention and deletion

Locally stored data remains on the device until you delete the relevant phrase/recording, reset data in the app where available, clear Parrot Trainer's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Phrase recordings are not retained by DimonSmart on a server because Parrot Trainer has no developer-operated backend.

Data processed by the Android speech-recognition or TTS provider is subject to that provider's retention and deletion policy.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact published on the Parrot Trainer Google Play listing. You can also submit a privacy inquiry through the public project issue tracker: https://github.com/DimonSmart/ParrotTrainer/issues
