# Parrot Trainer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Parrot Trainer uses the microphone to detect sounds during training. You may
also record your own training phrases. These audio recordings, along with app
settings and training statistics, are stored locally in the app's private
storage on your device.

When you choose to record a phrase, Parrot Trainer can use Android's system
speech-recognition service to turn speech into text. The Android speech
recognition provider selected on your device may process audio according to its
own privacy policy. Parrot Trainer does not operate a backend and does not send
your recordings to its own server.

The app does not show advertising, sell personal data, or include analytics or
crash-reporting SDKs. You can remove locally stored phrases and recordings from
the phrase editor, or remove all app data in Android settings.

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support address shown
on the Google Play listing.
