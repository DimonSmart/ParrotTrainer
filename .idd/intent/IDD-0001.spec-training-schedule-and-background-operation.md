# IDD-0001 spec: Training schedule and background operation

## Behavior

The owner can optionally limit training to a daily time interval. The main training switch represents whether Parrot Trainer is enabled by the owner and can be turned on at any time, including outside the configured interval.

When Parrot Trainer is enabled outside an active interval, it remains armed but does not listen for training events or speak to the parrot. It starts the active training session automatically when the interval begins. When the interval ends, the active session pauses automatically without turning the main switch off, and training resumes again at the next interval start. Turning the main switch off stops the active session, if any, and prevents later automatic starts.

The owner can allow the phone screen to turn off while Parrot Trainer is enabled. When this option is enabled, the Android foreground service remains active while the main switch is on, including while waiting for the next scheduled interval, so the app can resume training automatically in the background. Android shows its required persistent notification. When the option is disabled, active training keeps the screen awake and pauses on backgrounding until the app returns to the foreground.

Both schedule choices and the interval are stored locally. By default, the time interval is disabled and the screen may turn off during training.

## Acceptance criteria

- The owner can enable or disable the daily interval and choose its start and end times.
- The main training switch can be turned on both inside and outside the configured interval.
- When enabled outside the interval, the main switch remains on while the active training session remains stopped.
- An enabled trainer starts the active session automatically when the interval begins.
- When the interval ends, the active session stops without disabling the main training switch.
- An enabled trainer starts again at the next interval without another user action.
- Turning the main training switch off prevents any automatic restart.
- An enabled interval that crosses midnight is supported.
- The owner can choose whether training keeps the screen awake.
- Enabled background operation is represented by an Android foreground-service notification, including while waiting for the next scheduled interval.

## Verification

- Check time-interval boundaries, including an interval that crosses midnight, with automated settings tests.
- Check controller behavior for enabled-but-outside-schedule, automatic activation when the schedule gate opens, pausing when it closes, and explicit disable.
- On an Android device, enable Parrot Trainer outside the allowed interval with screen-off operation enabled, turn the screen off, and confirm that the foreground-service notification remains visible and training begins when the allowed interval starts.
