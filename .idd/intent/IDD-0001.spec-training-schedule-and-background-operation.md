# IDD-0001 spec: Training schedule and background operation

## Behavior

The owner can optionally limit training with a recurring daily schedule mask made of 96 independent 15-minute slots. The mask repeats every day and may contain any number of separate active intervals, including intervals that cross midnight.

The main training switch and the active training session are distinct states. Turning the main switch off prevents training entirely. Turning it on expresses the owner's intent to train, even when the current schedule slot is disabled. In a disabled slot the program remains enabled but does not listen or speak. When the schedule reaches an enabled slot, the active session starts automatically; when it reaches a disabled slot, the active session stops automatically without changing the main switch.

The schedule is edited directly as a 24-hour grid with four quarter-hour cells per hour. Tapping a cell toggles it. Dragging across cells paints the whole gesture ON or OFF according to the first cell. The grid updates during the gesture and persists the final mask once when the gesture ends; there is no Save/Apply confirmation step.

The schedule mask and the choice to enforce it are stored locally. A new installation starts with schedule enforcement disabled and a default mask allowing 09:00 through 21:00. Legacy persisted start/end settings migrate automatically to the equivalent 15-minute mask, rounding outward so previously allowed time is not lost.

The owner can allow the phone screen to turn off during an active training session. When this option is enabled, active training continues while the app is in the background and Android shows its required persistent notification. When the option is disabled, active training keeps the screen awake and pauses on backgrounding; the main training intent remains separate from this active-session lifecycle.

## Acceptance criteria

- The owner can independently enable or disable any of the 96 daily 15-minute slots.
- The schedule can represent multiple independent active intervals and intervals crossing midnight.
- Tap toggles one schedule cell; a drag paints every crossed cell consistently ON or OFF.
- Schedule edits are visible during the gesture and are persisted once when the gesture ends or is cancelled.
- The main training switch can remain ON while the current schedule slot is OFF.
- An enabled main switch automatically starts and stops the active session when schedule state changes, without changing the main switch.
- Disabling schedule enforcement allows training at any time while preserving the stored mask for later reuse.
- All-OFF and all-ON masks are valid states; a uniform mask needs no transition timer.
- Legacy start/end settings migrate automatically, including midnight-crossing and equal-boundary cases.
- The owner can choose whether active training keeps the screen awake.
- Active background training is represented by an Android foreground-service notification.

## Verification

- Unit-test the 96-slot mask, quarter-hour boundaries, arbitrary non-adjacent slots, all-ON/all-OFF masks, next transitions within a day and across midnight, and JSON round trips.
- Unit-test legacy start/end migration, including outward rounding, midnight crossing, and equal boundaries.
- Widget-test tap, ON/OFF drag painting, repeated pointer movement, revisiting cells, interpolation over fast movement, and one persistence callback per gesture.
- Controller-test that the main switch stays ON while schedule transitions stop and restart the active session, and that switching the trainer OFF prevents later automatic starts.
- Run Flutter analysis, automated tests, and the Android release build.
