# IDD-0001 spec: Training schedule and background operation

## Behavior

The owner can optionally limit training to a daily time interval. Training that is active when the interval ends stops automatically. Outside an enabled interval, training cannot be started.

The owner can allow the phone screen to turn off during active training. When this option is enabled, active training continues while the app is in the background and Android shows its required persistent notification. When the option is disabled, training keeps the screen awake and stops on backgrounding.

Both choices and the interval are stored locally. By default, the time interval is disabled and the screen may turn off during training.

## Acceptance criteria

- The owner can enable or disable the daily interval and choose its start and end times.
- An enabled interval that crosses midnight is supported.
- An active session stops when it reaches the end of an enabled interval.
- The owner can choose whether training keeps the screen awake.
- Active background training is represented by an Android foreground-service notification.

## Verification

- Check time-interval boundaries, including an interval that crosses midnight, with automated settings tests.
- On an Android device, start training with screen-off operation enabled, turn the screen off, and confirm that sound detection and phrase playback continue while the notification is visible.
